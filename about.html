Our Story

From a bond of brotherhood to a shared dream.

AnuRAAGAM began with a dream envisioned by Chaitanya. During that journey, his bond with his senior, Avinash, grew far beyond a typical junior–senior relationship. Over the years, they became like brothers, united by mutual trust, respect, and an unwavering love for music. Together with Band Laya, they joined hands to transform that dream into a movement that brings people together through unforgettable musical experiences.

How It Began

A dream that became a brotherhood. A brotherhood that became a vision.

What started as Chaitanya's vision to create a unique musical community soon found its greatest strength in Avinash and Band Laya. Their relationship was never just that of a junior and senior—it evolved into a bond that felt like family. Together, they believed music could do more than entertain; it could connect hearts, create opportunities, and build a community that people would proudly call their own.

Today, AnuRAAGAM stands as the result of that shared vision. Every event reflects the trust, friendship, and passion that brought this collaboration to life. With every performance, every audience, and every artist who steps onto the stage, the dream continues to grow into something much bigger than either of them imagined.

What We Stand For
  • Brotherhood. Great communities are built on trust, respect, and genuine relationships.
  • Music Without Boundaries. Every voice deserves a stage and every listener deserves an unforgettable experience.
  • Building a Legacy. We aren't just organizing events—we're building a community that will inspire music lovers for years to come.
