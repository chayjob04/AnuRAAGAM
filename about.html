Our Story

Where friendship became a shared vision.

AnuRAAGAM began as the dream of Chaitanya, inspired by a simple belief that music has the power to bring people together. As the vision grew, it found its perfect partner in Avinash and Band Laya. What started as a friendship built on trust and mutual respect soon evolved into a collaboration with one common goal—to create something truly unforgettable for every music lover.

How It Began

A dream, a friendship, and one shared mission.

AnuRAAGAM was envisioned by Chaitanya with the ambition of creating more than just musical events. The goal was to build a community where artists, audiences, and music enthusiasts could come together to celebrate music in its purest form.

As the idea took shape, Avinash and Band Laya joined hands with AnuRAAGAM, transforming a personal dream into a collaborative journey. Together, they continue to build experiences that inspire talent, strengthen communities, and create unforgettable moments through the universal language of music.

What We Stand For
  • Music Unites. Every event should bring people together beyond differences.
  • Every Talent Matters. We believe every artist deserves an opportunity to perform and grow.
  • Building a Legacy. AnuRAAGAM isn't just about organizing events—it's about creating a movement that celebrates music, friendship, and community.
